# Natural conversations and the morning Gazette

The owner approved one focused change: let conversation emerge from personalities and replies, make published newspaper articles optional village context, and let the Gazette sound like a newspaper while preserving evidence and attribution. This changes application guidance and a bounded public-data projection. It does not alter hosted instructions, publish an edition, generate a paid evaluation, change production or create a newspaper-reading world event.

## Conversation and editorial guidance

The Director no longer receives a requirement to explore a fresh subject whenever there is nothing new to add. It may continue a familiar subject, return to an unfinished thread, drift, disagree or remain quiet. The required 1–64-character `topic` remains descriptive archive metadata; its schema description explicitly says it is not an assigned agenda. Output contract shapes, strict alternation, configurable message count, 160-character speech limit, original-word persistence, moderation, world-action validation and relationship accounting stay unchanged.

The Gazette prompt asks for natural reporting verbs such as “suggested,” “offered” and “imagined,” with a specific clarification only where needed to prevent a misleading inference. It avoids repeated stock explanations that no event occurred. The page's general evidence caveat stays in place. Citation requirements, source authorization, original quote reconstruction, claim types and publication controls are unchanged. The obsolete claim that every draft will receive human factual review is removed from generation instructions; the separate manual/automatic publication policy determines authority and the public page continues to identify that authority truthfully.

No paid model samples were run. Offline tests prove input/output boundaries and storage behavior, not improved literary quality or how often a model will choose a story.

## Optional published reporting

For an opportunity's frozen timestamp, the SQLite context provider checks at most the previous two completed America/New_York edition dates. Initially this supports published editorial v2 editions, whether human-reviewed or authorized under the automatic policy. Historical compiled v1 papers remain readable and unchanged; they are not silently upgraded or used as an editorial context source. Drafts, partial today previews, future publication timestamps, stale editions and unavailable/withdrawn sources do not supply context. No missing paper is generated, fetched over HTTP or synthesized as a fallback.

The provider uses the public reader's manifest, draft, approval/moderation, exact-source and current-visibility checks. It offers at most two complete stories, with a combined 4096-byte JSON ceiling. It prefers stories whose actual cited speakers include the current pair, with stable published-order ties; it does not infer interests from names or assign a topic. Oversized complete stories are omitted rather than truncating prose or dropping their qualifying paragraphs. The context includes the original headline, all paragraphs and displayed quotes, edition timestamp/hash, article index and original public citation IDs. It omits full third-party transcripts, source aliases, private attempt/accounting/approval metadata and unpublished material.

Reporting is explicitly optional untrusted data. Residents may ignore, discuss, question or joke about it. Publication/exposure does not establish reading, belief, agreement, attendance, commitments or completed actions. Reporting is attributed to the Gazette and does not become firsthand participant history. Newspaper material receives the first removal priority under the existing total context-byte guard, before personal dialogue, memories or identity. A fully dropped story creates no retained exposure receipt. This adds no tools, provider generations, moderation calls or budget increases.

## Provenance and feedback

After validation and moderation, the server attaches a receipt for the exact articles actually retained in the input. During the existing canonical conversation transaction it rechecks the immutable published revision, current public sources, complete article hashes, exact original citation IDs and the actual saved participant pair. It inserts one private `settings` receipt named `director_gazette_context:RUN_ID`. The receipt contains public provenance identifiers and hashes, not article prose, keys or private prompts. A changed/withdrawn source rejects application and rolls back conversation, memories, relationship changes and receipt together; dispatched accounting stays held. Quiet, failed, rejected, replayed or rolled-back work creates no accepted-conversation exposure.

The receipt records optional context supply and `influence_verified=false`; it is not an assertion that anyone read or used an article. It creates no resident memories, goals, transmission receipts or fact-source aliases. Any original words later retained as Director notes remain ordinary unverified quote notes.

The Gazette's bounded conversation-candidate batches can see a small provenance annotation that an accepted conversation had prior newspaper context. They receive the earlier edition date/hash and explicit unverified influence status, not private exposure details or withdrawn original source contents. Editor guidance says that repetition of earlier reporting is not independent confirmation and must retain attribution/dates. This preserves possible feedback lineage; it cannot prove whether a model sentence was actually influenced by an article or establish semantic factual entailment.

## Input/session compatibility

Fresh input is versioned `sqlite-v6-4`; existing output contracts remain v1/v2. Session scope includes the input version and a digest of the exact retained reporting receipt (or null). Identical pair/identity/contract/reporting scope reuses the usual bounded session. A new revision, changed retained selection or absent paper uses its own scope, so a previously supplied article session is not reused when its reporting is unavailable or removed. Earlier sessions/fingerprints remain intact; no reset, deletion, backfill or hosted-agent edit occurs. Normal managed creation/submission stays within existing reserved HTTP headroom and one encounter submission; actual input costs/cache behavior may differ and are not inferred from synthetic byte counts.

## Release and verification

There is no schema migration, new ENV setting, service, timer, credential, browser request or individual newspaper-delivery write. Only successful canonical conversations add the bounded settings provenance record. Normal deployment is owner-controlled. The installed Gazette worker/helper/owner code are physical-release-pinned: use the existing reviewed owner-workflow update procedure to install/pin the same new release while the Gazette editor is drained, preserving its limits, key, automatic-publication policy and prior timer state. Do not alter the Director timer or database permissions. No production release, installation, timer operation, migration or paid call is performed during development.

Require all 27 offline suites and exact-commit Ubuntu CI. Regressions cover natural guidance/schema metadata, both API adapters, read-only preparation, public-only complete articles, byte pressure, stale/future/withdrawn omission, no third-party memory aliases, exact session reuse/isolation, accepted-context provenance, rollback on source changes and optional reporting lineage in later source packs. Existing Director/Gazette tests continue to verify exact speech, moderation, history compatibility, daily/monthly caps, held uncertainty, no duplicate dispatch and private/public separation.

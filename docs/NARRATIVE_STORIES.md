# Director-authored narrative stories

Individual public conversations remain immutable. Existing short-term continuation receipts remain separate and retain their existing behavior; they are not automatically converted into narrative stories. The optional narrative capability defaults off and requires its own explicit, additive V6 migration on a backed-up, drained database. Migration creates empty append-only story and membership tables; it does not change user_version or backfill historical relationships.

When explicitly enabled in Director dialogue mode, the existing generation request carries a bounded set of candidate stories and an optional structured decision: none, start, continue, conclude, or revisit. Candidates are selected by actual public participation, not a PHP topic match. Each known prior exchange is attributed to a current participant who actually spoke; the other participant must not be assumed to know it. Only stories offered in the retained context can be selected. A participant may join an existing story when at least one current participant has an authorized prior exchange. Independent Spark mode and custom context providers fail closed while the capability is enabled.

PHP validates the decision and rechecks the offered story head, public source and actual holder under the existing successful-conversation transaction. Only a completed, moderated conversation can add membership. A conclusion is recorded with an accepted exchange; a later accepted exchange may explicitly revisit it. Quiet, rejected, failed, uncertain, rolled-back, and standalone outcomes create no story link. Membership, transition and ordering records are immutable. No new provider request, scheduler interval, budget, or historical conversation is created or modified.

The archive lists Director-linked stories separately from individual exchanges. Native links open a story and page through up to ten public exchanges at a time, in accepted order, with original speaker names, avatars, words, timestamps, sources and individual archive links. Lists are paginated by ten stories. Public authorization is rechecked for every visible exchange; private or invalid entries and their internal sequence gaps are not exposed. Existing archive filters and individual links remain available. Threads are not inferred from names, dates, topics or short-term lineage.

Local testing uses disposable databases and mock transports only. The migration and `LITTLE_SPARKS_NARRATIVE_STORIES_ENABLED=1` are separate future owner operations; this change does not run a production migration, change production configuration, replace the hosted Director definition, or authorize deployment or paid calls. Provider schema compatibility has only been tested offline.
